Development Phase in Progress

The development phase of LiveCricket is in progress. This means the website, at its current state, won't be fully functional. Certain features are either unstable or unusable. It's very likely that you'll come across unresolved bugs, but if you do please contact the development team to look into the issue.


Why LiveCricket?

LiveCricket is a fast, user-friendly scoring platform trusted by leading schools and clubs. It allows you to effortlessly access scores and results, or even score a game yourself.

About LiveCricket

LiveCricket was built to make cricket scoring simple, accurate, and easily accessible for schools, clubs and fans. Driven by our passion for cricket, we created a platform that keeps everyone connected with real-time updates and statistics.
